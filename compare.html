What APK Editor does not do

APK Editor needs Android 10 or later. It does not translate apps automatically, does not edit apps that are running, and does not remove licence checks, in-app purchase protection or signature checks.

Where the others do more

APK Explorer & Editor runs on Android 6 and later, and its source code is public. Apktool M runs on Android 4 and later, has a file manager and can translate an app's text into over 100 languages automatically.
